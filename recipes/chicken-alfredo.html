Chicken Alfredo

Description

A rich, creamy pasta dish featuring tender chicken, garlic, parmesan, and a silky alfredo sauce. Simple to make and perfect for a comforting meal.

Chicken Alfredo Ingredients

  • Fettuccine pasta
  • Chicken breast, sliced
  • Butter
  • Heavy cream
  • Garlic, minced
  • Parmesan cheese, grated
  • Olive oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • Parsley (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the fettuccine.
  2. Sauté the chicken until golden.
  3. Melt butter and cook garlic.
  4. Add heavy cream and simmer.
  5. Stir in parmesan until smooth.
  6. Toss pasta and chicken with the sauce.
  7. Serve and garnish with parsley.
