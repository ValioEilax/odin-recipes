Beef Tacos

Description

Simple and delicious beef tacos made with seasoned ground beef, fresh toppings, and warm tortillas. A quick meal that never disappoints.

Beef Taco Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Taco seasoning
  • Small flour or corn tortillas
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Diced tomatoes
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Onion, diced
  • Sour cream
  • Salsa
  • Olive oil
  • Salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oil and cook the ground beef.
  2. Add taco seasoning and a splash of water.
  3. Warm the tortillas.
  4. Assemble tacos with beef and toppings.
  5. Serve immediately.
