Lasagna Ingredients

  • Ground beef (or Italian sausage)
  • Onion, diced
  • Garlic, minced
  • Tomato sauce
  • Crushed tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Italian seasoning
  • Salt & pepper
  • Olive oil
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Parsley or basil
  • Lasagna noodles

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta sheets.
  2. Make the meat sauce.
  3. Mix the cheese filling.
  4. Assemble the lasagna.
  5. Bake.
  6. Let it rest before serving.
